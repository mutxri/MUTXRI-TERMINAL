GDP · level, growth and per capita by country
Gross domestic product for the four markets covered by the terminal, year by year, from the World Bank. The level is GDP at current prices, in US dollars. Growth is the annual percentage change. Per capita is GDP per person, in current US dollars. Every value is the published figure, reproduced as the source reports it. A year with no published value shows n/a and is never filled, and no value is carried forward.

Interest rates · monetary policy by country
The policy rates each central bank publishes, under the name that bank itself uses. A rate is printed only where the source file carries it, always with its own as-of date and source, and the published path is drawn as a step chart: the rate holds until the next published change. Egypt runs a corridor, so more than one rate is shown for a country rather than one merged number. Where the file carries no rate, the panel says so and gives the reason.

Currency · exchange rates against the US dollar
The local currency units one US dollar buys, for each of the four markets, taken from the cross-rate matrix the source file publishes. Every card states what the file does and does not carry: the rate, the quote timestamp, the source and the derivation. Where the file gives no timestamp, the panel shows the timestamp slot as not stated instead of a date it does not have, because a rate with an invented time is worse than a rate with no time.
