Interest rates · monetary policy by country
The policy rates each central bank publishes, under the name that bank itself uses. A rate is printed only where the source file carries it, always with its own as-of date and source, and the published path is drawn as a step chart: the rate holds until the next published change. Egypt runs a corridor, so more than one rate is shown for a country rather than one merged number. Where the file carries no rate, the panel says so and gives the reason.

Unemployment, income and retail · per country, with the basis of each figure
Three indicators for the four markets. A national labour force survey and an ILO modelled estimate are different measures, so both are shown and each carries its own basis label, period and source: they are never merged into one number. National income is GDP and GNI at current prices in the country's own currency, named as such, nominal, with the year. Retail sales carry their base period and say whether the measure is volume or value. Where a country publishes no figure for a series, the panel says so with the reason rather than showing a blank.
