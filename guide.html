USER GUIDE

Drive the terminal in ninety seconds.

MX Terminal is keyboard first. You type a ticker, a panel code, or both, and the workspace follows. This page is the whole vocabulary, plus a tour of where everything sits.

The tour

Under two minutes, start to finish: the board, the market rail, the heatmap, the command line, statements, the news wire and the screener.

The layout

Five regions. Everything you type lands in one of them.

TOP BARExchange badge, search box, clock
TAPEScrolling prices for the exchange you are on
WATCHLISTEvery security on this exchange. Code, price, change. Click a row to load it.
STAGE
The panel you opened. Heatmap by default. Switch with the tabs above it or a command.
INFO RAILThe quote sheet for the selected security, and where command errors appear.
STATUS BARThe command line lives here, bottom right

The command line is the input box at the bottom right, the one marked "command". Click it, type, press Enter. Your text is not case sensitive.

What to type

Four patterns cover everything.

PatternExampleWhat happens
HELP HELP Lists every panel code in the info rail on the right.
A ticker SOL Opens that company's quote sheet in the info rail: price, change, volume, sector, country, chief executive, competitors, customers, partners, suppliers, shares in issue, market cap and major shareholders.
A panel code HEATMAP Opens that panel in the stage, on the exchange you are currently viewing.
Ticker then panel ABG FINANCIALS Opens the panel already scoped to that company. This is the fastest route to a single company's statements.

A company name works wherever a ticker does, if you type it in full. Anything the terminal cannot resolve comes back as a short error in the info rail rather than a blank screen.

Panel codes

Type any of these on their own, or after a ticker. They are also the tabs across the top of the stage.

CodePanelData
HEATMAPSector map for the exchange. Cell size is weight, colour is the day's move. Click a sector for its constituents, then a name to chart it in candles, line or area over 1D, 1M, 1Y, 10Y or the full history.live snapshot

GLCOGlobal commodities: gold, brent and the rest of the complex that moves these markets.live

TASTime and sales, the intraday print tape.not available
RATINGSAnalyst consensus and signal tally.no signals yet

Why some panels say that. These markets are end of day, so there is no intraday print tape to show under TAS, and no free analyst feed behind RATINGS. The terminal says so rather than filling the space with numbers nobody sourced. BND has real central bank auction data behind it, but the deployed panel is still pointed at a live endpoint that the static site does not serve, so it falls back to its labelled sample.
